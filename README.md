Hi! 👋 I'm Bianca, and I love rock climbing. Like many climbers, I also wonder **what makes climbers freakishly good.** 

In this project, I analyze a public survey of climbers to identify which physical characteristics, training habits, and climbing backgrounds are most strongly associated with climbing performance.

# Research Questions
This project explores questions such as:
1. What features most directly influence a climber’s max grade?
2. Similar/different physical characteristics of indoor vs. outdoor climbers? 
3. Are # of pull up reps associated with high V grades?
4. Are indoor + outdoor climbers more versatile?
5. What features are most predictive for women vs. men?

# Project Goals
1. Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA).
2. Build predictive machine learning models to identify gaps in training.
3. Create an informative resource for climbers who want to assess their climbing potential.


**Whether you climb everyday or know nothing about the sport, I hope you find something cool here!**
